## Before or when you hire

- **SSS**: register as an employer (Form R-1) and report each employee.
- **PhilHealth**: register as an employer (Form ER1).
- **Pag-IBIG**: submit the Employer's Data Form.
- **BIR**: add withholding tax on compensation to your registration using Form 1905 (to confirm the process with your RDO).

Each agency gives you an employer number; keep them on file and on your payroll records.

## Records to keep

- An employment contract or appointment for each employee.
- A payroll register for each pay period: gross pay, deductions, net pay.
- Payslips given to employees.
- Proof of each remittance to SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and the BIR.
