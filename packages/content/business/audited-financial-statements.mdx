## When an audit is required

Your books must be audited by an independent CPA when your gross quarterly sales, earnings, receipts or output exceed **₱3,000,000** (to confirm the threshold and how it is measured). Corporations generally attach audited financial statements to their annual return under separate rules (to confirm).

## What you file

- Attach the financial statements (audited where required) to your **annual income tax return**: Form 1701 for individuals who need them, Form 1702-RT for corporations on the regular rate.
- Electronic filing of financial statements through the BIR's eAFS system may apply (to confirm).

## Preparing for the audit

- Keep your books up to date and reconciled with the bank.
- Prepare schedules: receivables, payables, fixed assets and depreciation, taxes paid.
- Keep invoices, receipts and Forms 2307 organized by month.

## Smaller businesses

Below the threshold, individuals still keep books and may prepare simple financial statements themselves. The practice sets show how.
