## The permits

- **Barangay business clearance** from the barangay where the business operates.
- **Mayor's or business permit** from the city or municipal Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO).
- Supporting clearances the LGU may require: fire safety inspection, sanitary permit, zoning or locational clearance.

## Renewing every year

- Renew your permit and pay local business tax within the **first 20 days of January** (to confirm; LGUs can extend the deadline).
- Bring last year's permit, your gross sales or receipts for last year, and your updated barangay clearance (to confirm the LGU's list).
- Late renewal usually carries surcharges and interest set by the LGU.

## Tips

- Display your mayor's permit at your place of business.
- If you move, update your permit with the new LGU and your BIR registration (Form 1905).

## Legal basis
