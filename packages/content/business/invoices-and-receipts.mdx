## The sales invoice is now the main document

Under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act, you issue a **sales invoice** for every sale of goods **and** services. Official receipts are now supplementary documents (for example, to acknowledge a payment), not the main proof of a sale.

## Getting BIR-registered invoices

- Get an **Authority to Print** through an accredited printer, or register your computerized or electronic invoicing system.
- Do this before your first sale (see the one-time setup checklist).

## Displaying your registration

- Post your **Certificate of Registration (Form 2303)** and the BIR's **"Ask for an invoice"** notice where customers can see them.

## What to keep

- Your copies of every invoice issued, in number order, including cancelled ones.
- Supplier invoices for your purchases and expenses. If you are VAT-registered, input VAT can only be claimed with valid VAT invoices.
- Forms 2307 from clients who withheld tax from your payments.

## Common mistakes

- Selling without issuing an invoice.
- Using invoices from a closed or old registration.
- Throwing away cancelled invoices; keep them in the series.
