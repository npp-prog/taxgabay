## Every month

- **BIR Form 1601C**: remit the tax withheld from employees' compensation — on or before the 10th of the following month (15 January for December). eFPS filers follow their group's staggered dates.
- **SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG**: remit employee and employer shares according to each agency's schedule (to confirm the due dates for your employer number).

## Every year

- **BIR Form 2316**: give each employee their certificate of compensation and tax withheld for last year — by 31 January (to confirm).
- **BIR Form 1604C**: file the annual information return with the alphalist of employees — on or before 31 January.
- **Year-end adjustment**: annualize each employee's tax in the last payroll of the year and refund or collect the difference.

## Tips

- Pay on time even when no tax was withheld for the month; some returns must still be filed.
- Keep the payroll register and remittance receipts with your books.
