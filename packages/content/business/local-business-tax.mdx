## What it is

Cities and municipalities collect a business tax under their revenue code. It is separate from your BIR taxes.

## How it is computed

- **New businesses**: usually based on capitalization (the capital you declared when registering).
- **Renewals**: usually based on gross sales or receipts of the previous year (to confirm with your LGU's revenue code).
- Rates differ by LGU and by type of business.

## When to pay

- Pay **annually** when you renew in January, or in **quarterly installments** within the first 20 days of each quarter (to confirm the LGU's rules).

## Cooperatives

Cooperatives may be exempt from some local taxes under the Cooperative Code (to confirm).

## Tips

- Keep the LGU official receipts; local business tax is a deductible expense (Taxes and Licenses) for income tax.
