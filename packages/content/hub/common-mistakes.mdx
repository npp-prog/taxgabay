Most BIR penalties come from small, avoidable slips. Here are the most common ones.

## 1. Not filing because there were no sales

- **Instead:** file every return on your COR (Certificate of Registration, Form 2303) on time, even with zero sales. Late filing carries a surcharge and a compromise penalty even when no tax is due.

## 2. Missing the 8% election

- **Instead:** if you want the 8% option, choose it in your first quarterly return of the year (Form 1701Q) or at registration. It cannot be changed for the rest of the year.

## 3. Filing percentage tax while on the 8% option

- **Instead:** if you elected 8%, you do not file Form 2551Q — the 8% replaces percentage tax.

## 4. Deducting the ₱250,000 every quarter

- **Instead:** under the 8% option the ₱250,000 reduction is applied once, in the cumulative (year-to-date) computation — and only if you are purely self-employed.

## 5. Not claiming Form 2307 credits

- **Instead:** collect a Form 2307 from every client who withheld tax from you, and claim the credit in your income tax return.

## 6. Claiming input VAT without valid invoices

- **Instead:** only claim input VAT supported by valid VAT invoices in your name.

## 7. Using the wrong annual form

- **Instead:** use Form 1701A only if you earn purely from business or profession and use the 8% option or OSD. Mixed-income earners and those using itemized deductions file Form 1701.

## 8. Not updating books until filing time

- **Instead:** record sales and expenses as they happen. Registered books are required, and reconstructing them later is slow and error-prone.

## 9. Ignoring withholding duties

- **Instead:** if you have employees, withhold and file 1601C monthly. If you pay rent, professional fees or contractors subject to expanded withholding, file 0619E and 1601EQ and issue Form 2307.

## 10. Paying late without computing penalties

- **Instead:** if you are late, compute the surcharge and interest so you pay the right amount. Micro and small taxpayers get lower rates under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act. Use the penalty calculator.

## Legal basis

- NIRC Secs. 248 and 249, as amended by RA 10963 (TRAIN) and RA 11976 (EOPT) — surcharge and interest
- RR 8-2018 — 8% option
- NIRC Sec. 232 — books of accounts
