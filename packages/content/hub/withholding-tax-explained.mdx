Withholding tax means the payer deducts tax from a payment and sends it to the BIR on the payee's behalf. The payer is called a withholding agent.

## Withholding on compensation (employees)

- Employers deduct income tax from salaries every payroll, using the BIR withholding tax table.
- Remit monthly with Form 1601C: by the 10th of the following month (15 January for December).
- After the year ends, give each employee Form 2316 (Certificate of Compensation Payment/Tax Withheld) and file Form 1604C with the alphalist (list) of employees by 31 January.
- Employees with one employer for the whole year, whose tax was correctly withheld, usually qualify for substituted filing: Form 2316 serves as their annual return.

## Expanded withholding tax (EWT)

- A creditable tax withheld on certain payments to suppliers, such as rent, professional fees and payments to contractors. The rate depends on the ATC (alphanumeric tax code) of the payment — for example, 5% on rent.
- The payee can credit the tax withheld against their own income tax.
- Remit with Form 0619E for the first two months of each quarter (by the 10th of the following month) and Form 1601EQ for the quarter (by the last day of the month after the quarter), with the Quarterly Alphalist of Payees.
- Give the payee BIR Form 2307 (Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source). File Form 1604E with the annual alphalist of payees by 1 March.

## If clients withhold from you

- Collect Form 2307 from every client who withheld tax from your payments.
- Attach them to your quarterly and annual income tax returns to claim the credit, so you don't pay the same tax twice.

## Final withholding tax

- For some income, the tax withheld is final: the payee does not report it again in their income tax return. These are outside the scope of the TaxGabay calculators for now.
