Documentary stamp tax (DST) is a tax on documents such as loan agreements, shares of stock, leases and insurance policies. Form 2000 reports and pays DST on these documents month by month. For one-time transactions such as the sale of real property, use 2000-OT.

## Who must file

- Persons who make, sign, issue, accept or transfer documents subject to DST (for example lenders and borrowers on loan agreements, corporations issuing shares, lessors and lessees)
- Either party may pay, but both are liable if it is not paid (to confirm)

## Deadline

- Within **five days after the close of the month** in which the taxable document was made, signed, issued, accepted or transferred (to confirm).
- Taxpayers using the eDST system may follow different rules (to confirm).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms or eFPS.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm).

## Attachments

- Copies of the taxable documents, where required (to confirm)
- Summary list of the documents covered (to confirm)

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting DST on loan agreements and advances, including those between related companies.
- Missing DST on the original issue of shares when a corporation is formed or increases its capital.
- Using 2000 for a one-time sale of property (use 2000-OT).
