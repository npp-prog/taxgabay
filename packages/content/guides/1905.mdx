Use Form 1905 when your registration details change: moving to another RDO, adding tax types (for example withholding tax when you hire employees), correcting details, or closing your business.

## Who must file

- Registered taxpayers transferring to another RDO
- Taxpayers adding or removing tax types or updating registration details
- Taxpayers closing their business and cancelling their registration

## Deadline

- File before or when the change happens (exact timing to confirm per type of update).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** online through ORUS where available, or with your RDO.
- **Pay:** no tax is paid with this form.

## Attachments

- Certificate of Registration (Form 2303)
- Documents supporting the change (new address, proof of closure, list of ending inventory, unused invoices for cancellation, etc.)

## Common mistakes

- Moving the business without transferring the registration to the new RDO.
- Hiring employees without adding withholding tax on compensation to the registration.
- Stopping operations without cancelling the registration, so open returns keep accumulating penalties.
