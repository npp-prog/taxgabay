This is an information return; tax is not paid with it.

## Who must file

- Every employer that paid compensation during the year

## Deadline

- On or before **31 January** of the following year.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Attachments

- Alphalist of Employees (schedules for employees with and without substituted filing, terminated employees, minimum wage earners, etc.)

- Totals in the alphalist not matching the monthly 1601C remittances.
- Not issuing BIR Form 2316 to employees by 31 January (to confirm date).
- Leaving out employees who resigned during the year.
