Capital gains tax applies when you sell real property held as a capital asset (not used in business). The tax is a final tax of 6% of the highest of the selling price, the BIR zonal value and the assessor's fair market value.

## Who must file

- Individuals and corporations selling, exchanging or otherwise disposing of real property classified as a capital asset in the Philippines

## Deadline

- Within **30 days after the sale** (to confirm).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms where available, or with the RDO that has jurisdiction (for one-time transactions, usually where the property is located or where the decedent/donor resided — to confirm).
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm for one-time transactions, which may still be processed by the RDO where the property is located).

## Attachments

- Notarized deed of sale
- Certified true copies of the title and the latest tax declaration
- Proof of the zonal value and assessor's fair market value
- Other documents your RDO requires for the electronic Certificate Authorizing Registration (eCAR)

## Common mistakes

- Computing the tax on the selling price when the zonal or assessor's value is higher.
- Forgetting the documentary stamp tax (Form 2000-OT) on the same sale.
- Filing late: penalties run from the deadline even when the buyer has not yet transferred the title.
