The rate is 3% of gross quarterly sales or receipts, except 1% from 1 July 2020 to 30 June 2023 under CREATE.

## Who must file

- Non-VAT-registered persons whose gross annual sales/receipts do not exceed the VAT threshold and who did **not** elect the 8% income tax option
- Non-VAT corporations and partnerships (they cannot elect 8%)
- Others subject to percentage tax under NIRC Secs. 117–127 (e.g. certain carriers, franchise grantees) — outside Phase 1 scope

## Deadline

- Within **25 days after the end of each taxable quarter** (e.g. 25 April for January–March).
- Corporations on a fiscal year follow their own quarters.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Attachments

- Certificates of Creditable Tax Withheld (BIR Form 2307) for percentage tax withheld, if any
- Summary Alphalist of Withholding Taxes (SAWT), if claiming credits

## Common mistakes

- Filing 2551Q while on the 8% option — the 8% rate is in lieu of percentage tax.
- Using the old 1% rate after 30 June 2023 (it reverted to 3% from 1 July 2023).
- Not filing in quarters with zero sales.
