Before a printer can print your invoices (or receipts, for older stock), the BIR must issue an **Authority to Print (ATP)**. Form 1906 is the application.

## Who must file

- Newly registered businesses before they first print invoices
- Businesses that are running out of invoices and need a new batch
- Businesses that use computerized or e-invoicing systems may follow different rules (to confirm)

## Deadline

- Before printing — there is no fixed calendar deadline. Apply early enough that you never run out of invoices.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** online through ORUS where available, or with your RDO; often the BIR-accredited printer files for you (to confirm).
- **Pay:** no tax is paid with this form.

## Attachments

- Certificate of Registration (Form 2303)
- Sample of the invoice layout, if required (to confirm)
- Last booklet used, or a report of unused invoices, for re-printing (to confirm)

## Common mistakes

- Using a printer that is not accredited by the BIR.
- Continuing to issue old "official receipts" as the main sales document after the EOPT change to invoices without checking the transition rules (to confirm).
- Running out of invoices and issuing unregistered ones in the meantime.
