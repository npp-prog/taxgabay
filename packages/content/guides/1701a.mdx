The ₱250,000 reduction under the 8% option applies here because 1701A filers have no compensation income.

## Who must file

- Individuals earning purely from business or profession who use the **8% option**
- Individuals earning purely from business or profession on graduated rates with the **Optional Standard Deduction (OSD)**

## Deadline

- On or before **15 April** of the year after the taxable year.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Attachments

- Certificates of Creditable Tax Withheld (BIR Form 2307)
- Summary Alphalist of Withholding Taxes (SAWT)
- Financial statements are generally not required for OSD/8% filers, but keep your books (to confirm attachment rules)

## Common mistakes

- Using 1701A while having compensation income — mixed-income earners must use Form 1701.
- Using 1701A with itemized deductions — use Form 1701 instead.
- Not reconciling the annual figures with the three quarterly returns.
