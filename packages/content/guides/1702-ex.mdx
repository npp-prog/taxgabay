The annual return for corporations, partnerships and other organizations whose income is **exempt** from income tax under the Tax Code or a special law — for example qualified non-stock, non-profit organizations — and that have no income taxed at the regular or special rates.

## Who must file

- Organizations exempt under NIRC Sec. 30 (for example certain non-stock, non-profit charitable, religious or educational organizations — to confirm the qualifying conditions)
- Entities exempt under special laws, with proof of the exemption (to confirm)
- Not for you if any of your income is taxable — use 1702-RT or 1702-MX instead

## Deadline

- On or before the **15th day of the fourth month** after the close of the taxable year (for calendar-year organizations, 15 April).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms, or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** usually no income tax is due; penalties for late filing still apply (to confirm).

## Attachments

- Audited financial statements, where required (threshold to confirm)
- Proof of the tax exemption, such as a certificate or ruling, where required (to confirm)

## Common mistakes

- Treating all income as exempt: income from activities outside the exempt purpose, or from property, may be taxable under NIRC Sec. 30 (last paragraph) (to confirm).
- Not filing because no tax is due — exempt organizations still file the return.
- Letting the proof of exemption lapse.
