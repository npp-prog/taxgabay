Corporations pay income tax quarterly on a cumulative basis for the first three quarters of their taxable year; the fourth quarter is covered by the annual return (1702-RT, 1702-EX or 1702-MX).

## Who must file

- Domestic corporations, including One Person Corporations (OPCs)
- Partnerships (other than general professional partnerships) and other non-individual taxpayers

## Deadline

- Within **60 days after the close of each of the first three quarters** of the taxable year (calendar or fiscal).
- If the date falls on a weekend or holiday, file on the next working day. Your TaxGabay calendar does this automatically.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms, or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm for one-time transactions, which may still be processed by the RDO where the property is located).

## Attachments

- Certificates of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source (BIR Form 2307) received from customers
- Summary Alphalist of Withholding Taxes (SAWT), if claiming credits

## Common mistakes

- Computing the quarter on its own instead of cumulatively for the year to date.
- Forgetting the minimum corporate income tax (MCIT) comparison once it applies (from the 4th taxable year after operations began).
- Claiming the reduced 20% rate without checking both conditions (net taxable income ₱5M or less and total assets ₱100M or less, excluding land).
