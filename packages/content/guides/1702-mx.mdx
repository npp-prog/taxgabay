The annual return for corporations and partnerships with income taxed under **more than one regime** — for example a registered business enterprise with incentive income (exempt or at a special rate) and other income at the regular rate.

## Who must file

- Corporations with income subject to the regular rate **and** to a special or preferential rate, or exempt income (to confirm)
- Registered business enterprises (for example BOI- or PEZA-registered) with both registered and unregistered activities (to confirm)

## Deadline

- On or before the **15th day of the fourth month** after the close of the taxable year (for calendar-year corporations, 15 April).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms, or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm).

## Attachments

- Audited financial statements, where required (threshold to confirm)
- Separate schedules of income and expenses for each activity or tax regime (to confirm)
- Certificate of registration or entitlement to the incentive (to confirm)
- Certificates of Creditable Tax Withheld (BIR Form 2307) and the SAWT

## Common mistakes

- Not separating the costs of registered and unregistered activities, so incentive income is overstated.
- Claiming an incentive after it has expired.
- Filing 1702-RT for the whole income instead of 1702-MX.
