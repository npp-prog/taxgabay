Expanded withholding rates vary by ATC (alphanumeric tax code); the [expanded withholding tax calculator](/calculators/ewt) looks up the rate for common ATCs.

## Who must file

- Withholding agents who withheld expanded withholding tax (e.g. on rent, professional fees, payments to contractors, top withholding agents' purchases)

## Deadline

- For the **first two months of each quarter** only, on or before the **10th day of the following month** (eBIRForms).
- eFPS filers: staggered dates by group (to confirm).
- The third month of each quarter is covered by Form 1601EQ.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Common mistakes

- Filing 0619E for the third month of the quarter (use 1601EQ instead).
- Not issuing BIR Form 2307 to payees.
- Using the wrong ATC (alphanumeric tax code) and rate.
