Mixed-income earners combine compensation and business taxable income under graduated rates, unless they elected 8% for the business income (then compensation is taxed separately at graduated rates).

## Who must file

- Mixed-income earners (compensation plus business or professional income)
- Self-employed individuals and professionals who use **itemized deductions**
- Estates and trusts engaged in business

## Deadline

- On or before **15 April** of the year after the taxable year.
- Calendar-year basis is required for individuals.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Attachments

- Certificate of Compensation Payment/Tax Withheld (BIR Form 2316), for mixed-income earners
- Certificates of Creditable Tax Withheld (BIR Form 2307)
- Account Information Return / financial statements — audited if gross sales/receipts exceed the threshold under NIRC Sec. 232 (amount to confirm)
- Summary Alphalist of Withholding Taxes (SAWT)

## Common mistakes

- Mixed-income earners on the 8% option: the ₱250,000 reduction does **not** apply to the business income.
- Itemized deductions without supporting receipts and properly kept books.
- Leaving out compensation from a second employer.
