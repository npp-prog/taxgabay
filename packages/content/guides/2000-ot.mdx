Documentary stamp tax on the sale of real property is ₱15 for each ₱1,000 (or fraction of ₱1,000) of the higher of the consideration and the fair market value.

## Who must file

- Parties to a one-time taxable document such as a deed of sale of real property (by agreement, usually the seller or the buyer)

## Deadline

- Within **five days after the close of the month** in which the document was made, signed or issued.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms where available, or with the RDO that has jurisdiction (for one-time transactions, usually where the property is located or where the decedent/donor resided — to confirm).
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm for one-time transactions, which may still be processed by the RDO where the property is located).

## Attachments

- The taxable document (e.g. notarized deed of sale)
- Proof of the fair market value used

## Common mistakes

- Computing DST on the selling price when the fair market value is higher.
- Rounding down a fraction of ₱1,000 instead of counting it as a full ₱1,000.
- Filing on the regular monthly DST form instead of 2000-OT.
