The annual return for corporations and partnerships taxed only at the regular rate. Corporations that are exempt use 1702-EX, and those with income under both regular and special rates use 1702-MX (guides not yet written).

## Who must file

- Corporations, OPCs and partnerships subject only to the regular corporate income tax rate

## Deadline

- On or before the **15th day of the fourth month** after the close of the taxable year (for calendar-year corporations, 15 April).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms, or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm for one-time transactions, which may still be processed by the RDO where the property is located).

## Attachments

- Audited financial statements, where required (threshold to confirm)
- Certificates of Creditable Tax Withheld (BIR Form 2307) and the SAWT
- Proof of quarterly payments (1702Q)

## Common mistakes

- Paying regular corporate income tax when MCIT is higher (the higher of the two is due).
- Not carrying forward excess MCIT over RCIT, which can be credited against RCIT in the next three years.
- Using the wrong rate for a fiscal year that spans a rate change (pro-rating may be needed).
