Quarterly figures are **cumulative** (year to date). The tax due for the quarter is the cumulative tax less payments in prior quarters.

## Who must file

- Self-employed individuals and professionals (freelancers, doctors, lawyers, CPAs, etc.)
- Sole proprietors
- Mixed-income earners (employees who also have business or professional income), for the business portion

## Deadline

- Q1 (January–March): on or before **15 May**
- Q2 (April–June): on or before **15 August**
- Q3 (July–September): on or before **15 November**
- There is no Q4 1701Q — the fourth quarter is covered by the annual return (1701 or 1701A).
- If the date falls on a weekend or holiday, file on the next working day. Your TaxGabay calendar does this automatically.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Attachments

- Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source (BIR Form 2307), if clients withheld tax from your payments
- Summary Alphalist of Withholding Taxes (SAWT), if you claim creditable withholding tax
- Proof of prior quarter payments, if any

## Common mistakes

- Choosing the 8% option late: the election is made in the **first quarter** return (or at registration) and is irrevocable for the year.
- Deducting the ₱250,000 under the 8% option every quarter — it is deducted once, in the cumulative (year-to-date) computation.
- Not claiming 2307 credits, or claiming them without the SAWT.
- Filing 'no payment' returns late — late filing still carries penalties even with zero tax due.
