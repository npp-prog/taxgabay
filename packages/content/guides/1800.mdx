Donor's tax is 6% of the total gifts made during the calendar year in excess of ₱250,000. Each new gift is added to the earlier gifts of the same year, and donor's tax already paid that year is deducted.

## Who must file

- Any person (individual or entity) who transfers property or money by gift

## Deadline

- Within **30 days after the date of the gift**.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms where available, or with the RDO that has jurisdiction (for one-time transactions, usually where the property is located or where the decedent/donor resided — to confirm).
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm for one-time transactions, which may still be processed by the RDO where the property is located).

## Attachments

- Deed of donation
- Proof of the value of the property donated (for real property: zonal and assessor's values)
- Documents supporting any exempt gift

## Common mistakes

- Treating each gift separately instead of adding up all gifts in the calendar year.
- Forgetting to deduct donor's tax already paid on earlier gifts that year.
- Not filing when the gift is within the ₱250,000 exemption but the RDO still requires a return for the eCAR (to confirm).
