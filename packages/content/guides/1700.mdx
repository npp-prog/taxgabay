The annual return for employees whose only income is salary (compensation income). Most employees never file it: if your employer withheld the right tax, the Form 2316 your employer gives you takes its place — this is called **substituted filing**.

## Who must file

- Employees who had **two or more employers at the same time or one after another** in the same year (to confirm the exact conditions)
- Employees whose employer did not withhold the correct tax
- Employees who are not qualified for substituted filing for another reason (to confirm the full list)
- Not for you if you also earn from a business or profession — use 1701 or 1701A instead

## Deadline

- On or before **15 April** of the following year.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms, or with your RDO.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm).

## Attachments

- Certificates of Compensation Payment/Tax Withheld (BIR Form 2316) from **every** employer during the year
- Other documents supporting tax credits, if any (to confirm)

## Common mistakes

- Filing 1700 when substituted filing applies — not wrong, but not needed.
- Not filing when you changed jobs during the year and the new employer did not combine your income from the previous employer.
- Forgetting a Form 2316 from one employer, so income is understated.
- Using 1700 when you also have business or professional income.
