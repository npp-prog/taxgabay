Compensation withholding uses the revised withholding tax tables (RR 11-2018). Use the [withholding on compensation calculator](/calculators/withholding-compensation) for each payroll and for the year-end adjustment.

## Who must file

- Every employer (individual or juridical) that pays compensation to employees, even if no tax was withheld for the month

## Deadline

- eBIRForms/manual: on or before the **10th day of the following month**.
- December return: on or before **15 January** of the following year.
- eFPS filers: staggered by industry group — Group A 15th, B 14th, C 13th, D 12th, E 11th (to confirm).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Attachments

- None required on filing for most employers; keep payroll records and employee 2316s

## Common mistakes

- Not filing for months with no withholding (e.g. all employees below the taxable threshold).
- Remitting late after payroll — penalties run from the due date.
- Wrong eFPS group date.
