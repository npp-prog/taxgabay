Net capital gains from selling shares of stock not traded on the stock exchange are taxed at 15%.

## Who must file

- Individuals and corporations selling shares of stock not listed or not traded through the local stock exchange

## Deadline

- Within **30 days after the sale** (to confirm).

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms where available, or with the RDO that has jurisdiction (for one-time transactions, usually where the property is located or where the decedent/donor resided — to confirm).
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of an RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere — to confirm for one-time transactions, which may still be processed by the RDO where the property is located).

## Attachments

- Deed of sale or assignment of the shares
- Proof of the cost of the shares
- Latest audited financial statements of the company whose shares were sold (to support the fair market value)

## Common mistakes

- Selling below the fair market value of the shares (adjusted net asset value): the shortfall is treated as a gift subject to donor's tax.
- Leaving out selling expenses that reduce the gain, or including costs that are not allowed.
