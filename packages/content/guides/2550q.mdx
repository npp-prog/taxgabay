VAT payable = output VAT (12% of vatable sales) less creditable input VAT. Excess input VAT is carried over.

## Who must file

- VAT-registered persons (mandatory once gross sales/receipts exceed the VAT threshold, or voluntary registration)

## Deadline

- Within **25 days after the close of each taxable quarter**.
- Monthly VAT declarations (2550M) are no longer required from 1 January 2023.

## Where to file and pay

- **File:** eBIRForms (offline package or online), or eFPS if you are required or enrolled to use it.
- **Pay:** authorized agent banks, GCash/Maya/LANDBANK LinkBiz and other BIR-accredited e-payment channels, or the Revenue Collection Officer of any RDO (under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act you may file and pay anywhere).
- Keep the filing reference/confirmation email and the proof of payment.

## Attachments

- Summary List of Sales and Purchases (SLSP), as required
- Certificates of Creditable VAT Withheld (BIR Form 2307), if any
- Supporting schedules for input tax carried over

## Common mistakes

- Claiming input VAT without valid VAT invoices.
- Not separating VAT-exempt and zero-rated sales from vatable sales.
- Forgetting to carry over excess input VAT to the next quarter.
