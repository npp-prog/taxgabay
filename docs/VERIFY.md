## Modeling decisions to confirm

- **Penalty interest base** excludes the surcharge (simple interest on basic tax only), using 365 days per year, including leap years.
- **Interest rate change mid-period** (e.g. EOPT on 22 Jan 2024): interest is split per day by the rate in force on each day.
- **Surcharge for a violation before EOPT** uses the pre-EOPT rate (25%) even if paid after 22 Jan 2024, because the surcharge rule is picked on the day after the due date.
- **Willful (50%) surcharge** is not reduced for micro/small taxpayers.
- **Compromise penalty** is shown as a suggestion (it is not imposed by law) and only when tax is due; failure-to-file with no tax due is not covered.
- **Percentage tax in the income tax comparison** uses the year-end rate for the whole year (warning shown for 2020 and 2023).
- **Itemized deductions** are entered as one total; no NOLCO, no 1701 attachments.
- **Deadline roll-over** skips weekends, regular holidays **and special non-working days**.
- **1601C December** due 15 January applies to eFPS filers too.
- **Individuals** are forced to the calendar year; fiscal quarters apply only to non-individuals (2551Q, 2550Q, 1702Q, 1702-RT).
- **Cooperatives** get the same returns as other non-individuals (possible exemptions under RA 9520 not modeled).
